PLEASE READ BEFORE ORDERING

Our delivery & refund policies.

Clear terms for a smooth Nivara shopping experience.

1. No-Refund Policy

All purchases are considered final. Please review your product selection, fragrance finish, quantity and order details carefully before confirming your order.

This policy does not exclude any refund, replacement or other remedy required under applicable Kenyan law, including where an order is defective, incorrect or otherwise qualifies for a legal remedy.

2. Additional Transport Charges

Our standard delivery fees are KES 350 for approved nearby counties and KES 700 for selected farther destinations. Collection from our designated Nairobi pickup point is free.

If the actual transport cost for your destination exceeds the standard delivery fee, Nivara will contact you and explain the additional amount before dispatch. We will proceed only after you approve the extra charge.

If you do not approve the additional transport cost, we will discuss available alternatives with you before proceeding with the order. No additional transport charge will be added without your agreement.

3. Delivery Availability

County deliveries depend on an available suitable route and carrier acceptance of perfume. County orders are collected from an agreed parcel office or terminal; home delivery is not included unless separately agreed.
