NIVARA CUSTOMER POLICIES

Before you place your order.

Please review our policies carefully so you know what to expect when shopping with Nivara.

1. All Sales Are Final

Please carefully confirm your fragrance, bottle finish, quantity, delivery destination and order details before completing your purchase. We do not offer refunds or exchanges for change of mind or incorrect selections made by the customer, except where required by applicable law.

Nothing in this policy limits your legal rights. If an item arrives damaged, defective, incorrect or does not conform to your order, please contact Nivara promptly with your order details and, where possible, photographs. We will review the issue and provide any remedy required under Kenyan law.

2. Fixed Delivery Fees

Collection from Nivara's designated Nairobi pickup point is free. Delivery to approved nearby counties costs KES 350. Delivery to selected farther destinations costs KES 700.

County deliveries are collected from an agreed parcel office or bus/shuttle terminal. Home delivery is not included unless separately agreed.

3. Additional Transport Charges

Our standard delivery fees are fixed customer-facing rates. However, if the actual transport cost for your specific destination exceeds the standard fee, Nivara will contact you before dispatch, explain the additional amount and request your approval.

We will not add an additional transport charge without your agreement. If you do not approve the extra cost, we will discuss available alternatives with you before proceeding. No alternative arrangement or extra charge will be imposed without your agreement.

4. Delivery Availability

Delivery depends on a suitable route being available and the carrier accepting perfume for transport. We will confirm the delivery arrangement with you before dispatch. If the selected route is unavailable, we will contact you to discuss alternatives.

5. Need Help With an Order?

Please contact Nivara with your order number and a description of your concern. We will review your enquiry and explain the available next steps.
